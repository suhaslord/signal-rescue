How it works

A repeatable, synthetic map. Each user connects to a tower within range, subject to its capacity. The outage model chooses the nearest available tower. Balancing places users with fewer tower choices first. Range extension increases active towers’ reach by 35%; combined recovery tries both assignment methods and keeps the better result.

¹ Range cost is a relative proxy: sum of squared range multipliers, compared with the active network. No terrain, interference, handover timing, or real carrier data is modeled.

Runs entirely in your browser. An educational simulation, not a live coverage map.
