Simulated reach, not metres. Relay needs power and backhaul. Options use reassignment; repairs and relay use the current extension.

method

Users need coverage and a free tower slot. Reassignment moves connections to fit more users; extension increases simulated reach.

A selected area gets first access to available slots; other areas may lose connections. Repairs are ranked by connections restored. Relay search tests 144 locations. Range sweep uses 5% steps. Layouts are repeatable.

Inspired by rural connectivity in India. Simulation uses assumed coverage and backup duration; no terrain, interference or repair costs. Runs in your browser.
